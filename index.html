06 — Aftercare

Heal it
right

Half the tattoo happens in the chair. The other half is how you look after it. Tap a stage.

Fresh & tender

  • Keep the wrap on for as long as Rud tells you to.
  • Wash gently with lukewarm water and a mild, unscented soap.
  • Pat it dry with a clean paper towel. Don't rub.
  • Apply a very thin layer of the recommended balm.

The itchy stage

  • It will flake and peel. Don't pick or scratch it.
  • Keep moisturising, lightly, 2–3 times a day.
  • No swimming pools, rivers or long soaks.
  • Wear loose clothes over the tattoo.

Settling in

  • The skin may look a little cloudy or shiny. That's normal.
  • Keep it out of direct sun.
  • Gym and sweat are fine again once it stops peeling.
  • Message us if anything looks off.

Wear it for life

  • Sunscreen keeps the blacks deep and the greys crisp.
  • Drink water and moisturise. Healthy skin holds ink better.
  • Come back for a touch-up check when Rud recommends one.
  • Tag @rudraksha_tattooz when you show it off.
07 — Before you come

Questions,
answered

Do I need an appointment?

For anything bigger than a small piece, yes. A booking means Rud has time to draw your design properly. Small walk-ins happen when the chair is free, so call or WhatsApp first.

How much will my tattoo cost?

That depends on size, placement and detail. Send a reference and placement through the form below and you'll get an honest quote before anything starts.

Can you design something completely original?

That's how we prefer to work. Bring your idea, story or references, and the design is drawn from scratch for you.

Does it hurt?

A little. It depends on where it goes. Eat a proper meal, sleep well, skip alcohol the night before, and we'll take breaks whenever you need them.

Is there an age limit?

You must be 18 or older, with valid ID.
